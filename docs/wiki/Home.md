# Myna3D

Myna3D (formerly MynaMod, earlier Finch) is the **planned** 3D modelling app of the Myna suite: an independent clone of Blender 5.2.3 LTS with its own app template under `scripts/startup/bl_app_templates_system/oform/` (brand, icons, BIM tools). It is not built or shipped: no installer, not part of the suite builds.

There is no upstream remote and no merge or sync from Blender.
